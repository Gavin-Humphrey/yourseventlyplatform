When you enable Paid Ticketing on a Pro event, your public event page automatically transforms its RSVP form into a secure, multi-tier Stripe checkout flow with instant QR pass delivery.

Important Policy: 100% Independent of Your Host Plan

Our 5% platform charge per ticket applies strictly to paid tickets sold on any ticketed event and has nothing to do with which plan the host subscribed to.

  • ● Whether you unlocked ticketing via the $19 Pro One-Time Event pass or you are subscribed to Unlimited Pro, the platform fee on paid tickets is the exact same flat 5% per ticket sold.
  • ● Hosting a free-entry RSVP event (even on $19 Pro or Unlimited Pro)? You pay $0 (0%) in ticket fees—the 5% charge only ever applies when money is collected for a paid ticket.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Can I start with Free Bee and upgrade my event later?

Yes! Every host can create and publish events on Free Bee for $0. At any time—even after guests have already started RSVPing—you can edit your event, toggle on any ★ PRO feature, and upgrade that event to the $19 Pro One-Time Event plan or subscribe to Unlimited Pro without losing a single guest record.

Does subscribing to Unlimited Pro waive or change the 5% ticket fee?

No. The 5% platform charge per ticket applies strictly to paid ticket transactions on any ticketed event and has nothing to do with which host plan you subscribed to. Whether you paid $19 for a single Pro event or subscribe to Unlimited Pro, the per-ticket platform charge is a flat 5%.

Are all colors and layouts really available on Free Bee?

Yes. Giving every host full creative control over their event’s Page Background, Card/Section Background, Primary Accent, Typography colors, and all 8 layout structures (including Showcase, Summit, and Nightlife) is a core tradition of YoursEvently.

What happens if I buy a $19 Pro Event and later upgrade to Unlimited Pro?

Your $19 Pro Event remains permanently unlocked, and upgrading to Unlimited Pro immediately unlocks all ★ PRO features across every other event on your dashboard and every future event you create.
